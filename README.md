# Harvest-IQ

**"Smart Decisions. Better Harvests."**

Harvest-IQ is an intelligent web application designed to empower farmers by providing data-driven recommendations. The platform helps farmers optimize their crop yields through personalized crop, fertilizer, and disease management solutions based on real-time environmental data.

## Problem Statement

Farmers often face challenges in making informed decisions regarding crop selection, disease management, and fertilizer use. Many small-scale farmers lack access to timely, data-driven insights that can help them optimize their farming practices, leading to reduced crop yields, higher costs, and greater environmental impact. **Harvest-IQ** addresses this gap by offering smart, data-driven solutions for more effective farming decisions.

## Solution

Harvest-IQ leverages modern technologies, including machine learning and AI, to provide farmers with:

1. **Crop Recommendation System**: Recommends the most suitable crops based on environmental data, optimizing yield potential.
2. **Image-Based Disease Classification**: Uses deep learning to identify plant diseases from uploaded images and provides treatment suggestions.
3. **Fertilizer Recommendation System**: Recommends the best fertilizers based on the soil's nutrient composition, ensuring optimal growth conditions for crops.

## Features

### 1. Crop Recommendation
- **Description**: The system recommends the most suitable crop for a given field, based on environmental factors such as soil health and weather conditions.
- **Inputs**: Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P), Potassium (K), Temperature, Humidity, pH level, Rainfall.
- **Output**: The best crop recommendation based on the input data.

### 2. Image-Based Disease Classification
- **Description**: This feature leverages deep learning algorithms to classify plant diseases from images and provide accurate treatment suggestions.
- **Inputs**: Plant image (uploaded by the user).
- **Output**: Disease diagnosis and recommended treatments.

### 3. Fertilizer Recommendation
- **Description**: Based on the soil's nutrient levels, the system recommends the best fertilizers to improve crop health and yield.
- **Inputs**: Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P), Potassium (K), pH level, Moisture content.
- **Output**: Recommended fertilizer types and usage instructions.
